About HOTI

Our Mission

To empower young people ages 12-17 through innovative programs that foster creativity, build confidence, and develop essential life skills for success in the 21st century.

Our Vision

A world where every young person has access to transformative opportunities that unlock their creative potential and prepare them to make meaningful contributions to their communities.

Who We Serve

We focus specifically on youth between the ages of 12-17, a critical developmental period where young people are forming their identities and making important decisions about their futures.

Our Approach

We combine hands-on learning, mentorship, and peer collaboration to create engaging experiences that build both technical skills and emotional intelligence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you serve?

We specifically serve youth between the ages of 12-17. Our programs are designed for middle and high school students who are ready to explore their creative potential and develop valuable skills.

How much do programs cost?

All HOTI programs are completely free for participants. We believe that financial barriers should never prevent young people from accessing transformative educational opportunities.

How do I enroll in a program?

You can enroll by clicking the "Enroll Now" button on any program card or by contacting us directly. We'll guide you through the simple application process and help you choose the best program for your interests.

Do I need prior experience?

No prior experience is required! Our programs are designed to meet you where you are and help you grow. We welcome beginners and provide the support you need to succeed.

Where are programs held?

Programs are held at our main facility and various community partner locations. We also offer some virtual options. Specific locations are provided when you enroll in a program.

What should I bring to programs?

Just bring yourself and an open mind! We provide all necessary materials, equipment, and resources. You'll receive a detailed list of what to expect before your first session.
