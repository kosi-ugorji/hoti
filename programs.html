Innovative workshops and educational programs for young minds (Ages 12-17)

Design Thinking for Impact

Teach participants to identify real-world problems and create human-centered solutions

Curriculum

  • Introduction to design thinking
  • Empathy: understanding user needs
  • Defining the problem
  • Brainstorming and ideation techniques
  • Prototyping with simple tools
  • Testing and feedback loops
Duration:
4 weeks (2 sessions per week, 2 hours each)
Age Range:
12-17 years
Prerequisites:
No prior experience required
Capacity:
Limited to 30 participants per cohort
